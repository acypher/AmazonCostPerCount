# Amazon Results Sort by CostPerCount Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

Amazon Results Sort by CostPerCount is a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on Mac. It reorders the Amazon.com search results currently loaded in your browser by unit price.

The extension reads product titles and prices on Amazon.com search result pages to calculate unit prices. It changes only the page displayed in your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data or browsing activity. It makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

Your normal use of Amazon.com remains subject to Amazon's own privacy practices. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL.
