# Amazon Results Sort by CostPerCount Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

Amazon Results Sort by CostPerCount is a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on Mac. It reorders the Amazon.com search results currently loaded in your browser by unit price.

The extension reads product titles and prices on Amazon.com search result pages to calculate unit prices. It changes only the page displayed in your browser. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data or browsing activity. For ambiguous package quantities, it may download the matching public product image from Amazon’s image servers and read its printed quantity using bundled Tesseract OCR. Image recognition runs locally in your browser; no images, extracted text, titles, prices, or search terms are sent to an AI service. Image requests omit credentials. The extension has no analytics or accounts. Recognition code and language data are bundled, and do not download from a third-party service.

Your normal use of Amazon.com remains subject to Amazon's own privacy practices. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL.
